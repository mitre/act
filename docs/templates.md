# MITRE ACT™ Templates

The ACT process templates below are intended to be tailored and customized for your organization and your assessment scope - make the process work for you and your specific needs and goals.

## Handbook and fact sheet

- [MITRE ACT Assessment Handbook](/downloads/act-templates/individual-templates/G-01%20-%20MITRE%20ACT%20Assessment%20Handbook.docx){target="_self"} · downloadable Word version
- [MITRE ACT Fact Sheet](/downloads/mitre-act-fact-sheet.pdf){target="_self"} · One-page overview
- [Contact the ACT team](mailto:act@mitre.org) for help choosing or adapting a template.
