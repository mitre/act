# ACT in Practice

## Choose an assessment approach {#assessment-types}

- **Use existing evidence for a risk assessment.** Inventory what you have, such as compliance results, running configurations, incident records, threat reporting, and applicable requirements. Identify gaps that could affect the risk decision.
- **Add a security assessment if you need fresh evidence.** This optional track evaluates selected security requirements. Use evidence of both compliance and noncompliance in the risk assessment.

## Set the scope and rigor {#flexible-implementation}

Choose the systems, applications, and infrastructure to cover. Set one of ACT’s four levels of assessment rigor based on what the assessment needs to establish. In the disaster response example, the team would identify the personnel deployment portal, who depends on it, and which evidence it already has for authentication and access.

## Start planning {#getting-started-with-act}

1. Use the [Preliminary Intake Form (P-01)](/templates#plan-the-assessment) to capture the system’s mission, stakeholders, and available evidence.
2. Agree on the assessment boundary, scope, and rigor with the system team.
3. Record those decisions in the risk assessment plan or security assessment plan, depending on the work in scope.

Use the [planning templates](/templates#plan-the-assessment) to get started. See the [downloadable ACT Handbook](/templates#handbook-and-fact-sheet) for the full planning process.
